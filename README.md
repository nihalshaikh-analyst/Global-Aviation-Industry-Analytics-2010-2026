# 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Aviation Industry Analysis 2010–2026** is an end-to-end Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project that analyzes the global aviation industry using **PostgreSQL, SQL, Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI, and DAX**.

The project examines more than **2,000 aviation records** covering:

- ✈️ 30 major global airlines
- 📅 17 years of industry data
- 🛩️ Boeing, Airbus, COMAC, and Embraer aircraft data
- 🌍 Regional passenger traffic
- 🛣️ 40 major global aviation routes
- 🚨 40 major aviation incidents
- 🦠 COVID-19 aviation impact
- 🛫 Boeing 737 MAX crisis
- 📈 Airbus market growth
- 🇨🇳 COMAC C919 emergence
- 🇸🇦 Saudi aviation expansion
- 🌎 Global aviation recovery

The main objective of this project is to transform raw aviation datasets into a complete analytical solution and generate meaningful business insights.

# 🧮 Key DAX Measures

## Total Revenue

```DAX
Total Revenue =
SUM('Airline Financials'[revenue_usd_bn])
```

## Total Passengers

```DAX
Total Passengers =
SUM('Airline Financials'[passengers_carried_m])
```

## Average Load Factor

```DAX
Average Load Factor =
AVERAGE('Airline Financials'[load_factor_pct])
```

## Total Orders

```DAX
Total Orders =
SUM('Fleet Orders'[orders_net])
```

## Total Deliveries

```DAX
Total Deliveries =
SUM('Fleet Orders'[deliveries])
```

## Total Incidents

```DAX
Total Incidents =
COUNTROWS('Aviation Incidents')
```

## Total Fatalities

```DAX
Total Fatalities =
SUM('Aviation Incidents'[fatalities])
```

# 🔍 Business Insights

## 🦠 1. COVID-19 Had a Massive Impact on Aviation

COVID-19 caused a major decline in:

- Airline revenue
- Passenger traffic
- Load factor
- Operating margins
- Aircraft demand

The aviation industry experienced its largest disruption during 2020.

---

## 🌍 2. Recovery Was Different Across Regions

Recovery was not uniform.

```text
Middle East → Recovered Faster
North America → Strong Recovery
Europe → Gradual Recovery
Asia Pacific → Slower Recovery
```

The Middle East showed strong recovery due to global hub connectivity and international transit demand.

---

## 🛫 3. Boeing Faced Major Pressure

The Boeing 737 MAX crisis and later production challenges affected:

- Aircraft orders
- Deliveries
- Production
- Market confidence

Airbus gained significant momentum in the narrowbody aircraft market.

---

## 🛩️ 4. Airbus Strengthened Its Position

Airbus demonstrated strong order momentum, particularly in the A320 family.

The analysis shows Airbus benefiting from:

- Strong narrowbody demand
- Airline fleet replacement
- Boeing production challenges

---

## 🇨🇳 5. COMAC Is an Emerging Competitor

COMAC's C919 program shows increasing aircraft orders and deliveries.

The strongest growth opportunity currently exists in the Chinese domestic aviation market.

---

## 🇸🇦 6. Middle East Aviation Is Growing

Airlines such as:

- Emirates
- Qatar Airways
- Saudia
- Riyadh Air

represent growing competition in global hub aviation.

Saudi Arabia's aviation strategy could significantly reshape regional competition.

---

## 🛣️ 7. High Passenger Volume Does Not Always Mean High Revenue

Route analysis shows that:

```text
High Passengers ≠ Highest Revenue
```

Average fare and route distance also influence route revenue.

---

## 📈 8. Low-Cost Airlines Can Maintain Strong Margins

Business model analysis helps compare:

```text
Legacy Airlines
        vs
Low-Cost Airlines
        vs
Regional Airlines
```

Low-cost carriers can benefit from lower operating costs and efficient fleet utilization.

---

## 🚨 9. Aviation Safety and Major Incidents

The dataset tracks selected major aviation incidents between 2010 and 2026.

The analysis highlights:

- Fatal incidents
- Non-fatal incidents
- Geopolitical events
- Boeing-related incidents
- Airbus-related incidents

# ⚠️ Dataset Limitations

This project combines public reference data with modeled and interpolated values.

Therefore, results should be considered analytical estimates.

### Limitations

- Some airline financial values are estimated.
- Foreign airline revenues are converted to USD.
- Some values are interpolated between reference years.
- Route passenger volumes are estimated.
- Only selected major incidents are included.
- 2026 contains partial-year data.
- This dataset should not be used as the only source for investment or regulatory decisions.
