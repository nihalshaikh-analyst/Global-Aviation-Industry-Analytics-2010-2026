The DAX layer transforms the raw aviation data into meaningful business metrics.

- How much revenue did airlines generate?
- How quickly did revenue change?
- How many passengers were carried?
- How efficiently was capacity utilized?
- How many aircraft were ordered?
- How large is the aircraft backlog?
- Which airlines are leading by revenue?
- How severe were aviation incidents?
